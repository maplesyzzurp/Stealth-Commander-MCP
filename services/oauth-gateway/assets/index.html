Set Up Stealth Commander on Your PC (or Sign In to Your Account)

  
Sign in to your Stealth Commander account to auto-connect your linked Windows PCs, or run the command below in PowerShell on your Windows PC:

  stealth-commander setup --enable-remote
  
Account creation is open. The Windows client is currently distributed privately, and the ChatGPT plugin is not publicly listed. Select a PC paired to your signed-in account.
